    What ran this course

    Not something built into the agent. Three plain markdown files in your own project, which you can read and change:

    • course/agent/PROCEDURE.mdThe procedure. Which step you are on, when to hand you a line, when to stop and wait.
    • AGENTS.mdThe rules. One step at a time, evidence not impressions, never a confirmation you did not give.
    • README.mdThe curriculum. Every line you typed and every check you made came from here.

    Open PROCEDURE.md and read the pause points. It is ordinary English. The useful next move is writing one of these for a process you actually own — then when an agent goes off-pattern you point at the line instead of arguing with it.
